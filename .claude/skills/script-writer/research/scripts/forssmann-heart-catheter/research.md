## Short factual summary
In 1929 Werner Forssmann, a surgical resident aged 25, wanted a safer way to get life-saving drugs into the heart than stabbing a needle through the chest wall (intracardiac puncture). The fear at the time was that entering the heart would be fatal, and his superior refused permission. He numbed his own arm, inserted a urological catheter into an arm vein at the elbow, advanced it about 60 cm (65 cm catheter) and walked to the X-ray department, where a film showed its tip in his right atrium. He published in *Klinische Wochenschrift* (Nov 1929). He met ridicule, later turned to urology, and shared the 1956 Nobel Prize with Cournand and Richards, who developed the method from 1940 onward.

## Evidence buckets
### VERIFIED FACTS
- He was 25, his superior refused permission, the medical fear was that entering the heart would be fatal - Wikipedia, LITFL, Nobel bio
- Catheter inserted into an arm vein at the elbow (antecubital), advanced ~60 cm, X-ray showed tip in the right atrium - Nobel bio, Wikipedia, Siemens
- He walked to the X-ray department with the catheter in place - Nobel bio, Wikipedia
- He later practised urology (from 1950 in Bad Kreuznach) - Nobel bio, Wikipedia
- Shared the 1956 Nobel Prize (27 years later) - nobelprize.org
- Newspapers in 1956 portrayed him as an unknown rural physician - PubMed 25784449
- Cardiac catheterisation is a routine procedure today - general medical knowledge
### REPORTED CLAIMS (attribute)
- The nurse (Gerda Ditzen) agreed only if he tried it on her; he strapped her to the table, numbed her arm, then catheterised himself - from Forssmann's memoir via Wikipedia, Spektrum, IFLScience. Script: "According to his own memoir…"
- Sauerbruch's "circus" remark - attributed widely (Siemens MedMuseum, DGK archive); exact wording varies. Script: "reportedly called it a circus stunt"
### HISTORICAL DISPUTES
- Whether he was fired from Eberswalde/Charité and why - DGK "Dichtung und Wahrheit" paper argues the victim narrative is overstated. Script avoids "fired".
- Exact catheter length advanced (60 vs 65 cm). Script: "more than half a metre".
### POPULAR LEGENDS
