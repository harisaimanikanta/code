# Storyboard v1 - The Doctor Who Drank the Cure (for review)

- **Audio:** `voiceover.mp3` (ElevenLabs "Nathan", 87.7 s). Word timings are force-aligned offline (`word-timings.json`; all 230 words matched).
- **Look:** the Ricks Clips "Mechanism Parable" look (stylised round-head 3D characters in detailed sets, light that tells the time of day,
  a new image every ~1–1.5 s, 1–2-word captions) **+ Zack D. Films devices** (fly-throughs into the body, a microscopic world,
  clean single-object shots, x-ray and scale inserts).
- **Style frames:** `style-frames/overview.jpg` (lab hook · REJECTED · piglets · ammonia shield).
- **Format:** 1080×1920, 30 fps. Captions are drawn on screen and timed to the words.
- **Cast:** **Marshall**: young, brown hair tuft, white lab coat, blue shirt, red tie. **Warren**: grey side hair, glasses, moustache, lab coat.
  **Professor/reviewers**: bald, glasses, dark suits. **Piglets** ×3. **H. pylori**: green spiral bacterium with cartoon eyes and flagella.

## Accuracy guards
- No invented quotes on screen; the abstract title is paraphrased ("Spiral bacteria in the stomachs of patients with gastritis and ulcers"), not a real document.
- The conference banner is generic ("GASTRO CONFERENCE"), not the society's logo.
- Vomiting is comedic and non-graphic. The Nobel-stage scene is labelled a dramatization in the description.

## Production notes / limits
- Built procedurally in Three.js (shorts-renderer). Characters are stylised primitives: the Ricks Clips look, not Zack D.'s realistic humans.
- Sets are simplified versions of the reference look (fewer props and less texture detail). The quality ceiling is shown in the style frames.
- Estimated full render: ~2,630 frames × ~0.3–0.6 s ≈ 15–25 minutes.
