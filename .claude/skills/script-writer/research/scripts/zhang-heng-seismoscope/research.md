# Research: Zhang Heng's Earthquake Jar (the true version)

- **Slug:** zhang-heng-seismoscope · **Researched:** 2026-11-19 · **Verdict:** **GO** (core event in a primary text; mechanism disputed and said so)
- **Period:** Eastern Han dynasty; device presented 132 CE; the famous detection c. 138 CE
- **Place:** Luoyang (capital); earthquake in Longxi (modern Gansu), roughly 400–600 km away (sources vary)
- **Main people:** Zhang Heng (78–139 CE), court astronomer/polymath; "the scholars of the capital" (京师学者)

## Short factual summary
Zhang Heng presented a bronze vessel ringed by eight dragons, each holding a ball above an open-mouthed toad; a tremor released a
ball, and the dragon's direction indicated where the quake was. The *Book of Later Han* (Hou Hanshu, Zhang Heng biography) records
that once a dragon released its ball while no shaking was felt; the capital's scholars thought it strange that there was no sign;
days later a courier arrived reporting an earthquake in Longxi, and all acknowledged the device's marvel. Its internal mechanism is
described only briefly ("a central column", 都柱) and modern reconstructions disagree.

## Evidence buckets
**VERIFIED:** eight dragons/toads/balls design; ball dropped with no felt quake; capital scholars doubted (咸怪其无征 "all found it strange there was no sign"); courier days later (后数日驿至); quake in Longxi (果地震陇西); all were convinced (皆服其妙).
**REPORTED:** a sharp sound alerted observers (S3); "political enemies relished the failure" (Wikipedia via S2 - framing as *enemies* is secondary; script uses "scholars").
**DISPUTED:** exact distance (400 / 500 / 600 / 850 km across sources) → script says "hundreds of kilometers"; year 132 vs 138 (build vs event); whether reconstructions work (S2: replicas struggled; a recent Chinese project claims a working model, unverified).
**LEGEND / AVOID:** the competitor version's exile of the inventor, all eight balls falling, and the palace collapsing - **not in the record**.
**UNKNOWN:** exact internal mechanism; which dragon fired.

## Script-safety notes
- Plain fact: design; ball dropped; nobody felt it; doubters; courier days later; Longxi quake; everyone convinced; mechanism still debated.
- Dramatized (mark in description): the line "Where is this earthquake?" paraphrases 咸怪其无征; "world's fanciest false alarm" is narrator irony.
- Avoid: exile, rubble, numbers of km, "before anyone else on Earth".
