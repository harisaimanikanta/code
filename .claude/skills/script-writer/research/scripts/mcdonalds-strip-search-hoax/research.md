## Short factual summary
On 9 April 2004 a caller claiming to be "Officer Scott" phoned a McDonald's in Mount Washington, Kentucky, told assistant manager Donna Summers that a female employee matching his description had stolen a purse, and directed a search. Over more than three hours, at his direction, the 18-year-old was strip-searched and held; Summers took her clothes to her car. When Summers had to return to the counter, the caller asked for someone to watch her; one employee walked out; Summers called her fiancé, Walter Nix, who followed the caller's instructions and sexually abused the teenager. Maintenance man Thomas Simms refused the caller's demands. The call was a hoax; more than 70 similar calls had been reported in 30 states since about 1992. David R. Stewart, a Florida man traced through a calling card, was arrested and acquitted in 2006; the calls reportedly stopped after his arrest. Nix was sentenced to 5 years; the victim won about $6.1M against McDonald's (2007, upheld on appeal).
