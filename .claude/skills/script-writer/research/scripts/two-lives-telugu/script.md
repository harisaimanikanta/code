# రెండో జీవితం (Two Lives) - Shorts Package (Telugu, owner voiceover)

> Adapted use of this skill: Telugu motivational piece, voiced by the owner. The Retention Engine is applied in full. The English
> lints don't fit Telugu (they count syllables and words in English), so pace is measured at ~2.3 Telugu words/s.

## A. Story research
- **Title:** The two lives / what the dying regret · **Main character:** Bronnie Ware (palliative carer, Australia)
- **Summary:** Ware wrote down what patients in their last weeks regretted. The same five regrets kept coming back.
- **Verified:** the five regrets; blog → book (2011/12). **Reported (her words):** #1 most common; men said #2 and had missed
  their children growing up; "happiness is a choice" was realised only at the end.
- **Disputed:** the "two lives" quote's author (credited to Confucius without a source) → never credited in the voiceover.
- Sources: `research.md`.

## B. Hook options
**Question:** When does the "second life" begin, and what did dying people say they wished they'd done?
**Answer:** the day you realise you only have one life. **Surprise:** what they regretted was how they lived, not what they had,
and one regret was working too hard. **Planted detail:** the hook's "dying people told us". **Second reveal:** "happiness was a
choice", realised too late. Then it turns on the viewer: *you* still have time.

- 5–15 s · generic advice, no story · nothing new to learn, so viewers swipe in the middle · anchor on Bronnie Ware's documented regrets (true, concrete).
- 15–25 s · list of instructions ("చేయండి… చేయండి") · reads as "and then" · replace it with regrets that build to the worst one.
- Whole · ~42 s · under 60 s · the story beats bring it to ~70 s.
- Payoff · predictable · no surprise · surprise = "working too hard"; second reveal = "happiness was a choice".
