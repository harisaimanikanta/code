# Idea Engine - finding real stories that fit the True Mechanism Parable

The core method of the idea-creator skill. Output feeds the **script-writer** skill via IDEA CARDS.

## The 5-point fit test (score each 0–2; pitch only stories ≥ 7/10)
| # | Ingredient | 2 = strong | Why it matters (see reference-style-analysis.md) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Visual absurd action** to open on | "He fired gold into a mine wall with a shotgun" | Beat 1 needs a picture that makes no sense yet |
| 2 | **Documented doubt/mockery** | Rivals laughed, a court mocked him, the press nicknamed it | Beats 2 + 4 need real reactions to pay off |
| 3 | **Real reversal at peak stakes** | Proven right when it mattered most / backfired spectacularly | Beat 6 |
| 4 | **Physical, explainable mechanism** | A pendulum, heat expansion, a loophole, a psychological bias | Beat 7: the "how" is the prize |
| 5 | **Evidence quality** | Primary sources or solid scholarship for the central premise | Non-negotiable; 0 here = reject whatever the total |

## Where these stories live (search seeds)
- **Inventions doubted, then vindicated:** "inventor ridiculed", "dismissed at the time", "laughed at by the academy", "royal society rejected"
- **Plans that backfired:** "bounty backfired", "unintended consequences history", "perverse incentive", "cobra effect" (check: the Delhi cobra story itself is weakly sourced; the Hanoi rat bounty of 1902 is documented)
- **Cons with a clever mechanism:** salting, fake documents, forged art, insurance scams, "how the scam worked"
- **Engineering failures with a simple physics cause:** resonance, thermal expansion, fatigue, corrosion
- **Strange institutions that sound fake:** official practices, laws, rituals ("expulsion room", "dagen H")
- **Military deception:** inflatable armies, fake documents, decoys
Prefer sources: Smithsonian, BBC History, History Extra, National Archives, university press, Japan Times / NYT archives,
peer-reviewed journals. Use other Shorts only to check saturation (competition), never as evidence.

## IDEA CARD format (the handoff to script-writer, use exactly)
```
IDEA CARD
Title: <mechanism-curiosity title, e.g. "Why the Rat Bounty Made More Rats">
Category: <con | hoax | backfired plan | doubted invention | absurd war | impostor | trial | medical | engineering | espionage | other>
Era & place: <year(s), place>
Cold open: "He ..." (<= 17 words, visual, true)
True story: <2-3 sentences, only pre-checked facts>
Doubt beat: <who doubted/mocked, documented>
Reversal: <the real outcome>
Mechanism: <the how/why in 1-2 sentences>
Fit score: <x>/10 (action a, doubt b, reversal c, mechanism d, evidence e)
Competition: <low | medium | high | unknown> (<n> on-topic Shorts; top <views>; checked <date>, <method>)
Evidence: <1-3 best sources with URLs>
Watch out: <legends, disputed numbers, things not to claim>
Fresh angle: <what competitors miss, if the story is already covered>
```
Ideas are **leads**: script-writer still runs full research (its `historical-research-guidelines.md`) before writing.
