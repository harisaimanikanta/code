Why did doctors grind up Egyptian mummies and feed them to sick people?
For centuries, shops in Europe sold mummy powder as medicine... for bruises, coughs and even seizures.
You see, nobody set out to eat the dead.
Doctors actually wanted a medicine called mumia... a black, sticky tar that oozes out of rocks.
Persian doctors used it on wounds and broken bones.
But about nine hundred years ago... translators turning those books into Latin made a mistake.
They wrote that mumia came from inside embalmed bodies.
And some Egyptian bodies really were coated in black, sticky resin.
So Europe started eating the bodies instead.
Demand got so huge that some sellers faked mummies... from fresh corpses.
And a German drug company STILL sold mummy powder in nineteen twenty-four.
All because of ONE wrong word.
The medicine they wanted... was rock tar all along.
And the word mummy?
It first meant the medicine... not the body.
